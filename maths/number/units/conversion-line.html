The Conversion Line

Ten higher-tier GCSE questions on metric and imperial units, plus the two-step trick that solves every one of them.

How it works

  1. Find the bigger unit. Every question gives you a fact, like "2.2 pounds = 1 kilogram". Whichever unit needs more of itself to make one of the other is the bigger unit — here, it takes 2.2 pounds to make just 1 kilogram, so the kilogram is bigger and the pound is smaller.
  2. Pick the operation. Smaller unit into bigger unit: divide. Bigger unit into smaller unit: multiply.

Work out which way you're travelling — towards the bigger unit or the smaller one — then divide or multiply by the number in the question. That's the whole method.
