Formula & definition notebook
Radius / diameter
A radius joins the centre to the circumference. A diameter is a chord through the centre; d = 2r.
Chord / tangent
A chord joins two points on the circumference. A tangent touches the circle at one point and is perpendicular to the radius there.
Arc / sector / segment
An arc is part of the circumference. A sector is bounded by two radii and an arc. A segment is bounded by a chord and an arc.
Circumference
C = 2πr = πd. Length units, such as cm.
Area
A = πr². Square units, such as cm².
Arc length
L = (θ / 360) × 2πr.
Sector
A = (θ / 360) × πr². Perimeter = arc length + 2r.
Segment area
Minor segment = minor sector − triangle; triangle area = ½r² sin θ (calculator in degrees).
Circle theorems
Angle at centre = twice angle at circumference standing on the same arc. Angles in the same segment are equal. Angle in a semicircle = 90°. Opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral sum to 180°. Tangents from one external point have equal lengths. The perpendicular from the centre bisects a chord. Alternate segment theorem: the angle between a tangent and a chord equals the angle in the alternate segment.
