Reverse-engineering your example

Equilateral triangle: sides labelled 6x − 8 and 2x + 12. Find the perimeter.

  1. All sides are equal: 6x − 8 = 2x + 12.
  2. Subtract 2x and add 8: 4x = 20, so x = 5.
  3. Substitute: 6(5) − 8 = 22 cm. Check: 2(5) + 12 = 22 cm.
  4. Perimeter: 3 × 22 = 66 cm.

Common trap: 5 is the value of x, not the side length; 22 is one side, not the perimeter.
